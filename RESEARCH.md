# UNFORM — T-Shirt Competitive Research

## Competitor 1: YoungLA
- Website: https://www.youngla.com/?srsltid=AYCX9vIRbDZKvmrQNkDngmqLqprQc3R_v2lUAeryTo4BMBarSC2_VMIO 
- Date visited: 11/09/26
- Observation: YoungLA is a successful company that makes clothing for lifters. Their clothing is tailored to people who like hitting the gym. Oversized clothes is especially in style and many customers use these clothes outside of the gym as well.
- What this suggests: Currently, consumers, especially younger generations, enjoy the fit of oversized clothing, and I believe that even a white oversized t-shirt can sell pretty well. YoungLA also shows a sense of identity, which if UNFORM does the same, can be successful.

## Competitor 2: Jaded London
- Website: https://jadedldn.com/en-us?srsltid=AYCX9vIiAYWLMDQZoQTQtDzF8MLIngH5HPTmutn8j1Z4o-oB9tNM9jjI 
- Date visited: 11/09/26
- Observation: Jaded London is very experimental in their fashion which I think attracts a lot of consumers. 
- What this suggests: Younger generations love the thought of being different and aesthetic, especially in a world where trends are so prominent, making everyone seem the same. UNFORM can apply this bold and experimental style to attract customers. 

## Competitor 3: Uniqlo
- Website: https://www.uniqlo.com/us/en/?srsltid=AYCX9vIqzFA3-QDvBJ00GinJbdQFloCfwXTU3xn8eU4g0XgT5SIbxOvN 
- Date visited: 11/09/26
- Observation: Very minimalistic, good quality, and great fits. Uniqlo is modern yet so simple, it is a place where people enjoy buying basics that go with every outfit.
- What this suggests: Since UNFORM is working on basic white t-shirts, it can apply Uniqlo's strategy in being minimalistic yet modern. 

## Brand Positioning
- Position 1: Modernist Streetwear
- Position 2: Postmodernist Streetwear
- Chosen direction and why: Postmodernist Streetwear because I would like to tailor UNFORM to younger audiences who like to experiment with fashion and feel different. 

## Customer Personas
- Persona 1: Person in their 20s who appreciates modern fashion
- Persona 2: Person in their 40s who prefers traditional clothing
- Chosen audience and why: Persona 1, the website is postmodernist, representing a bold and experimental vibe. 

## Brand Character and Persuasion
- Visual style: Dark backgrounds, experimental typography, oversized silhouettes, and high-contrast imagery.
- Brand archetype: Outlaw
- Honest persuasion techniques: UNFORM can use authentic customer reviews (social proof), high-quality product photography, and detailed sizing information to build trust.

## Assumptions and Open Questions
- Assumptions: Oversized clothing and website aesthetic will attract Gen Z and Gen Alpha individuals. 
- Questions to investigate: What would a reasonable price range be? Will people actually like the website design? Will they think it is too much?
