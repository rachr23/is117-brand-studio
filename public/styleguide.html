06 / Voice

Voice traits

07 / Useful persuasion

Make the next
step honest.

Support a voluntary choice with information, not pressure.

Reciprocity

The page gives the visitor the color, fictional price, available demo sizes, and product-claim boundary before asking for a bag action. The benefit is a more informed choice.

Commitment and consistency

Selecting a size is a small, voluntary commitment that makes the next action specific: add that chosen size to a temporary demo bag. The page confirms the result and never implies an order or payment.
